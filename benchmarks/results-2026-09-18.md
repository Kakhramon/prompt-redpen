# redpen benchmark, 2026-09-18
`python3 benchmarks/run.py --reps 2`. 5 vague prompts, 2 rep(s) each, on a small sample repo. Every figure is `total_cost_usd` as the CLI reported it.
## Arm 1: what a vague prompt costs
| | mean cost | mean wall |
|---|--:|--:|
| letting `sonnet` guess at it | $0.1913 | 47.3s |
| redpen catching it on haiku | $0.0302 | 9.1s |
Catching costs 16% of guessing.
## Arm 2: what the wrong tier costs
| model | mean cost |
|---|--:|
| opus | $0.2698 |
| haiku | $0.0352 |
Same finished edit, 7.7x the price.
## What this does not show
Arm 1 measures the first pass only. A guess is not always waste, and a
caught prompt still has to be re-sent and paid for. The saving is the
wasted exploration, not the task.
Arm 2 assumes the small model finishes the task correctly. Check the
diffs before believing the ratio.
Sample is small: 2 rep(s). Treat these as an order of magnitude.
